FAQ

Honest answers before you search.

Will every prospect have an email?

No. GlobaLeads22 works with public contact data, so availability depends on what each business makes visible online.

Are these exact decision makers?

No. The app identifies likely decision makers based on public evidence like team pages, titles, profiles, and contact context.

Why do some searches return fewer prospects?

Because weak company-only records are filtered instead of being presented as finished opportunities.

Can I export prospects?

Paid users can export visible prospect results.

Who is this for?

Small agencies, freelancers, consultants, founders, and sales operators doing local or niche outbound.

Is this a CRM?

It is a focused prospecting and sales workspace. CRM-style organization exists, but the core job is building better local opportunity lists faster.
