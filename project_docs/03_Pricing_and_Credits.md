## What Users Get Per Search

All searches aim to return person-first leads with:

- Person name.
- Company / business name.
- Website when available.
- Phone when available.
- Public email when available.
- Social links when available.
- Valid person LinkedIn profile when available.
- Lead quality score.
- Lead quality label.
- Likely decision-maker card.

Search depth changes how hard the app searches:

- Simple: smaller run, cheaper, good for quick testing.
- Normal: balanced default for useful lists.
- Deep: wider search, more website scans, more attempts to find people.

Enrich mode spends more credits because it performs extra contact discovery and enrichment work.

## Competitor Pricing Notes

Pricing changes often. Verify before publishing public comparisons.

High-level market:

- Apollo: broader sales intelligence and engagement platform; paid tiers commonly start around small-team monthly subscriptions and climb with credits/features.
- Clay: workflow and enrichment platform; tends to price around credits, automations, and team workflows.
- PhantomBuster: automation tool with plans based on execution time/slots rather than lead quality.
- Outscraper-like tools: often pay-as-you-go or subscription pricing for raw Google Maps/business data extraction.
- Local lead gen tools: often cheaper but usually return raw company rows, not a focused person-first workflow.

GlobaLeads22 should avoid competing as "cheapest data scraper." The better positioning is a focused person-first lead research workflow for agencies and founders.
