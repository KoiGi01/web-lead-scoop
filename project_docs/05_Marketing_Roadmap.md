# Marketing Roadmap

## Goal

Get the first paid users by positioning GlobaLeads22 as a fast, person-first lead research tool for agencies, freelancers, and founders doing outbound.

## SEO Plan

Primary SEO angle:

- Person-first local B2B lead generation.
- Lead lists by niche and city.
- Alternatives to expensive sales databases.
- Agency prospecting workflows.

Initial content clusters:

- "How to find [industry] leads in [city/country]"
- "Best way to find local business decision makers"
- "How agencies can build outbound lead lists"
- "Apollo alternatives for local lead generation"
- "How to find business owner emails publicly"
- "Lead generation for web design agencies"
- "Lead generation for SEO agencies"

Landing page improvements:

- Clear founder offer at top.
- Clear pricing.
- Show person + company as mandatory lead outcome.
- Avoid provider/tooling language.
- Add concrete examples by niche: dentists, restaurants, law firms, clinics, agencies, real estate offices.

Programmatic SEO idea:

- City + niche pages, but only after the product and search quality are stable.
- Example: "Find dental clinic leads in Lisbon".
- Each page should explain the workflow, not pretend to host a static database.

## LinkedIn Strategy

Target:

- Agency owners.
- Freelancers.
- B2B consultants.
- Startup founders.
- SDR/team leads.

Post ideas:

- Before/after: manual research vs GlobaLeads22.
- "Company-only lead lists are not enough. You need the person name."
- Short teardown of bad lead lists.
- Build-in-public updates on search quality.
- Founder offer announcement.
- Screenshots of person-first leads with sensitive data blurred.
- Use case posts: "I searched dental clinics in Mexico and got X person-qualified leads."

Outbound approach:

- Message agency owners with a very direct offer.
- Ask them to test one search for their niche.
- Offer founder seat discount.
- Avoid long pitches.

## TikTok / Short-Form Ideas

Video formats:

- Screen recording: keyword + city to leads.
- "I tried to build a lead list manually. It took forever."
- "Why company names are useless for cold calls."
- "Watch me find who to ask for at local businesses."
- Funny comparison: bad lead list vs person-first lead list.
- Founder journey: fixing search quality in public.

Hooks:

- "If your lead list has no person names, it is not a lead list."
- "Stop cold calling companies when you do not know who to ask for."
- "I built a tool that finds local B2B leads by niche and city."
- "Here is how agencies can build a prospect list in minutes."

## Paid Ad Experiments

Start small and test clarity before scaling.

Instagram / Meta:

- Use bold visual concepts.
- Focus on pain: "Need leads, not another expensive tool?"
- Founder offer: first 20 seats.
- Target agency owners, marketers, freelancers, entrepreneurs.

Google Search:

- Bid on high-intent terms:
  - lead generation software.
  - local business leads.
  - find business emails.
  - B2B lead list.
  - agency lead generation.
  - Apollo alternative.
- Avoid claiming to be a scraper.
- Send traffic to a focused landing page with pricing and founder offer.

Ad test angles:

- "Find person-first B2B leads by niche + city."
- "Build local lead lists in minutes."
- "Stop researching. Start outreach."
- "First 20 founder seats get 50% off."

## Product Hunt Plan

Do Product Hunt after:

- Payment flow is stable.
- Search quality is reliable across 3-5 popular niches.
- Onboarding is polished.
- Contact/bug report path exists.
- Landing page has strong founder offer and pricing.

Launch assets:

- Short demo video.
- Before/after screenshots.
- Founder story.
- Clear positioning: person-first local B2B leads.
- Founder discount.

## First 100 Users Plan

First 20:

- Founder seats.
- Direct outreach to agency owners.
- Manual onboarding calls.
- Ask each user what niche they sell to.
- Run example searches with them.

Next 30:

- LinkedIn and short-form content.
- Small paid tests.
- Ask first users for testimonials.
- Build niche-specific landing pages.

Next 50:

- Referral offer.
- Publish use-case content.
- Add integrations or workflow features that repeated users ask for.
- Improve retention through saved searches, follow-ups, and pipeline.
