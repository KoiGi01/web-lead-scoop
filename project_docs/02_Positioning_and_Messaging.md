# Positioning And Messaging

## One-Liner

GlobaLeads22 helps sales teams and agencies find person-first B2B leads by niche and location in minutes.

## Short Positioning

GlobaLeads22 turns a keyword and location into a ranked list of businesses, contacts, public emails, phone numbers, websites, and likely decision makers, so users can start outreach without spending hours researching.

## Pain Points

- Manual prospect research takes too long.
- Existing tools return companies but not people.
- Local lead lists are often stale, generic, or missing contact context.
- Users do not know who to ask for when calling.
- Generic emails are not enough.
- Large sales platforms feel expensive or bloated.
- Scraping workflows are too technical for non-technical users.
- Exporting useful data is annoying.
- Users need quick tests of new markets and niches.

## Value Proposition

GlobaLeads22 helps users quickly build outreach-ready lead lists by combining business discovery, public website scanning, person discovery, contact extraction, lead scoring, and export in one focused workflow.

Core value:

- Faster prospecting.
- Person-first results.
- Local and niche targeting.
- Ranked leads instead of raw data dumps.
- Simple credit-based usage.
- CRM-style organization after leads are found.

## Words To Avoid

Avoid words that make the product sound like a low-quality scraper or a compliance risk:

- Google Maps scraper.
- Scraper.
- Scraping bot.
- Data harvesting.
- Mass harvesting.
- Spam tool.
- Guaranteed decision maker.
- Guaranteed emails.
- Private data.
- Bypass.
- Steal leads.
- Extract hidden emails.
- Unlimited scraping.
- Automated spam.

## Messaging Rules

- Say "public contact data" instead of "scraped data" in customer-facing copy.
- Say "likely decision maker" instead of "decision maker" when confidence is inferred.
- Say "ranked leads" instead of "raw scraped rows".
- Do not mention provider names in public copy unless required by a technical or legal context.
- Keep the promise practical: fast research help, not magic.
- Emphasize person + company as the minimum useful lead.
