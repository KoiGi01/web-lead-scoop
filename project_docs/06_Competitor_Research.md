## Apollo

What they do:

- Large B2B contact database.
- Sales intelligence.
- Email sequencing.
- Dialer/workflows.
- CRM integrations.
- Enrichment and buyer intent features on higher tiers.

What they do better:

- Huge database.
- Mature sales workflow.
- Strong integrations.
- Recognized brand.
- Good for established sales teams.

What they do worse for GlobaLeads22's target wedge:

- Can feel expensive or heavy for small agencies/founders.
- Less focused on local niche searches.
- Users may still need to filter and clean lists.
- Not positioned around quick person-first local lead discovery.

Pricing notes:

- Apollo has a free tier and paid monthly/annual plans.
- Paid tiers vary by credits, users, sequences, and advanced features.
- Treat Apollo as a broader sales platform, not a direct small local-lead workflow competitor.

## Clay

What they do:

- Data enrichment workflows.
- AI research agents.
- Waterfall enrichment.
- Integrations with many data providers.
- Spreadsheet-like prospecting workflows.

What they do better:

- Very powerful for advanced operators.
- Flexible enrichment workflows.
- Strong for complex outbound systems.
- Good ecosystem of data sources.

What they do worse for GlobaLeads22's target wedge:

- Can be intimidating for simpler users.
- Requires setup and workflow design.
- Users often need to understand data providers and enrichment logic.
- Less instantly focused on "keyword + city -> leads".

Pricing notes:

- Clay pricing is credit/workspace based.
- Paid plans are generally positioned above lightweight solo tools.
- Clay is a premium workflow/enrichment competitor, not a simple local prospecting app.

## PhantomBuster

What they do:

- Browser/cloud automation.
- LinkedIn, social, and web automations.
- Prebuilt "phantoms" for scraping/extraction workflows.
- Execution-time based automation plans.

What they do better:

- Flexible automations.
- Many prebuilt extraction recipes.
- Useful for technical growth operators.

What they do worse:

- More automation-tool than finished lead research workflow.
- Users need to configure tasks and understand limits.
- Output quality depends heavily on setup.
- Less focused on scoring and person-first lead acceptance.

Pricing notes:

- Plans are typically based on execution capacity and automation limits.
- Not priced around a simple per-search lead quality promise.

## Outscraper-Like Tools

What they do:

- Extract business listings and public business data.
- Often focus on maps/business directory data.
- Usually provide raw rows, APIs, or CSV exports.

What they do better:

- Good for bulk extraction.
- Often cheaper for raw data volume.
- Simple for users who only need company rows.

What they do worse:

- Raw company data is not enough for cold calling.
- Person names are often missing.
- Lead scoring and CRM workflow are usually limited.
- Users still need to clean, enrich, and organize the data.

Pricing notes:

- Often pay-as-you-go or subscription/credit models.
- Compete on extraction volume and raw data access.
- GlobaLeads22 should not position against them as "another scraper"; the wedge is person-first lead research plus sales workflow.

## Prospeo.io

What they do:

- People search and company search.
- Email finder and contact enrichment.
- Verified work emails and mobile numbers.
- CSV enrichment for existing lead files.
- Chrome extension for prospecting from webpages.
- API access for search/enrichment workflows.
- Integrations with CRM and outreach tools.
- Filters for person, company, job title, seniority, department, location, company size, industry, revenue, funding, technology, job changes, buying intent, and contact availability.

What they do better:

- Very strong person-first database positioning.
- Mature filter set for prospecting.
- Clear email/mobile reveal workflow.
- Strong enrichment angle for uploaded CSVs.
- CRM/sequencer sync and API are already part of the product story.
- Their help docs claim high email verification accuracy and emphasize deliverability.
- Credits are spent when contact details are revealed/exported/enriched, which can feel fair because users can browse before spending.

What they do worse for GlobaLeads22's target wedge:

- More like a B2B database and enrichment platform than a simple local lead research workflow.
- Users still need to know how to filter and build the right prospecting query.
- Local small-business discovery by keyword + city is not the obvious core use case.
- It may be stronger for LinkedIn-style/person database prospecting than finding local businesses from public web footprints.
- It can feel closer to Apollo/Clay territory, while GlobaLeads22 can stay simpler and more focused.

Pricing notes:

- Official help docs list Free, Starter, Growth, and Pro tiers.
- Their help docs show monthly credits per user: Free 100, Starter 2,000, Growth 5,000, Pro 15,000.
- Credits are used to reveal, export, or enrich email/mobile data.
- Unused credits do not roll over at the end of a billing cycle.
- Add-on credits are available for paid plans.
- Current dollar pricing should be checked directly on their pricing page before publishing comparisons.

Product notes:

- Prospeo is one of the closer strategic competitors because it is person-first.
- The strongest GlobaLeads22 differentiation should be the "keyword + location -> outreach-ready local leads" workflow, not generic email finding.
- Do not fight Prospeo on database size. Fight on speed, simplicity, local/niche search, lead scoring, and built-in sales workspace.

## Lead Scraping / Enrichment Tools

Examples:

- Local database scrapers.
- Email finder tools.
- Waterfall enrichment platforms.
- Website/contact extractors.
- LinkedIn automation tools.

What they do better:

- Some have deeper single-source coverage.
- Some have stronger email-finding infrastructure.
- Some have mature bulk APIs.

What they do worse:

- Users often stitch multiple tools together.
- Raw data is not always outreach-ready.
- Local niche workflows can be clunky.
- Many tools return generic emails without person context.

## Local Lead Gen Tools

What they do:

- Provide local business lead lists.
- Sometimes sell niche or city-specific lists.
- Sometimes provide CRM/export features.

What they do better:

- Simple buying experience.
- Some offer done-for-you list building.
- Can be cheaper if the user needs one static list.

What they do worse:

- Lists can be stale.
- Usually less interactive.
- Less transparent on freshness and evidence.
- Often company-first rather than person-first.

## GlobaLeads22 Differentiation

Core wedge:

- Person-first local/niche lead discovery.
- Keyword + location workflow.
- Public contact extraction and lead scoring.
- Built-in sales workspace.
- Credits that map to search effort.

Do not compete as:

- A Google Maps scraper.
- A raw data extractor.
- A generic enrichment spreadsheet.
- A spam automation tool.

Compete as:

- The fastest way for agencies and founders to build outreach-ready local B2B lead lists with actual people attached.
