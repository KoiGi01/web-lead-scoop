# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Freelancers, consultants, small agencies (web design, SEO, AI automation, paid ads, social media, reputation, CRM setup, lead gen) and B2B service providers running local outbound. They arrive mid-prospecting: they sell a service and need businesses worth pitching, the right person to contact, and a place to organize follow-up. They are operators who value speed and control, not enterprise sales teams who want a giant database. Non-technical enough that provider/tooling jargon should stay out of the UI.

## Product Purpose

GlobaLeads22 turns *service + target market + location* into a ranked list of prospects with public contact data and likely decision-maker contacts, so the user can start outreach in minutes instead of researching for hours. It is evolving from a lead-research tool into an opportunity-based prospecting agent — "find prospects with a visible reason to buy." Success = the user runs a scan and immediately has high-quality, person-level leads they can contact and track through a pipeline.

## Brand Personality

Premium, sharp, operator-grade — an *instrument*, not a toy. Confident and technical with zero marketing fluff. Three words: **precise · electric · operator-grade**. The interface should evoke control and momentum: the feeling of a well-made tool in capable hands.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS templates and component-library defaults. Pastel / purple-gradient-on-white startup aesthetics. Boring stock admin dashboards. Anything that reads as a "cheap Google-Maps-scraper" utility. Marketing fluff and cute/playful styling. Washed-out low-contrast gray text "for elegance."

## Design Principles

1. **Quality is the product.** Never ship a worse option as a choice (no "normal vs enrich"). The user tunes *what / who / where / how many* — never quality.
2. **Evidence over claims.** Show ranked prospects, real numbers, reasons to act — not adjectives.
3. **Calm instrument.** Density only where it earns attention; restraint everywhere else. One hot accent for what matters.
4. **One decision per step.** Remove jargon knobs (strictness, per-signal filters). Defaults the agent handles.
5. **Identity-preserving.** Near-black canvas + a single citron accent + mono labels. Evolve the craft; don't drift toward generic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Dark-only for v1 (light mode deferred). Target WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 against near-black surfaces — watch muted-gray text, which is the easiest contrast to fail here. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` (count-ups, ring fills, and sparkline draw are gated). Full keyboard navigation with a visible citron focus ring. Don't encode meaning in color alone — pair semantic colors (hot/warm/mint) with labels or icons.
